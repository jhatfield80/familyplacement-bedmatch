Frequently Asked Questions

What is BedMatch Family Placement?

BedMatch Family Placement connects medically fragile children with qualified foster families who have the training and resources to provide specialized medical care in a loving home environment.

Who can become a medical foster parent?

Medical foster parents need specialized training in pediatric care, medication administration, and medical equipment. We work with licensed agencies to ensure families meet all state requirements and receive ongoing support.

How does the matching process work?

Our platform uses advanced algorithms to match children's medical needs with families who have the appropriate training, experience, and resources. Case managers review all matches to ensure the best fit for both child and family.

Is BedMatch Family Placement available nationwide?

Yes, BedMatch Family Placement serves families and agencies across the United States, with specialized support for complex medical placements in all 50 states.

How much does BedMatch cost?

Pricing is customized based on agency size and placement volume. Contact us for a personalized quote and demo of the platform.

Is the platform HIPAA compliant?

Yes, BedMatch is fully HIPAA compliant with enterprise-grade encryption, secure data storage, and strict access controls to protect sensitive medical and personal information.
